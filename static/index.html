🪰 How FlyJack Works

Inspired by computational neuroscience projects (like Mark Unthank's FlyHard in CARLA), FlyJack connects the biological Drosophila melanogaster (fruit fly) connectome directly to a casino Blackjack table.

  • Sensory Input: Blackjack cards are translated into olfactory spike frequencies entering the fly's antennal lobe glomeruli (DA1, VA1d, VA1v).
  • Biological Reservoir: The 140,000-neuron connectome remains completely frozen; recurrent Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) dynamics extract high-dimensional features.
  • Embodied Decision: Descending motor neurons actuate the fly's physical foreleg gestures (tapping the felt for HIT, waving for STAND).
